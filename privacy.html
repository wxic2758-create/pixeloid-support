Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Summary

Pixeloid does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal data. Camera capture, photo import, style processing, and card export take place locally on your device.

Photos and camera data

The app accesses only the camera or photos you choose through the iOS camera and photo-picker interfaces. Access is used solely to create previews and exported cards. Your images are not uploaded to a server.

On-device preferences

The app stores preferences and card details locally, including selected style, aspect ratio, custom card names, and reroll history. This information remains on your device.

Analytics, tracking, and advertising

Pixeloid does not include analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, or third-party data collection SDKs.

Network access

The app does not upload your photos or card data to a server. Its core features work offline.

Children's privacy

Because Pixeloid does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the new practices take effect.
